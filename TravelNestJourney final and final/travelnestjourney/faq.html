Frequently Asked Questions

Home / FAQ
How do I book a tour package?

Simply browse our Packages page, select the tour you like, click 'Book This Package', and follow the 4-step booking process: trip details, traveler info, payment, and confirmation.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bKash, Nagad, Visa/Mastercard, and direct bank transfer. All payments are processed securely.

Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?

Yes, free cancellation is available up to 7 days before departure. Please contact our support team for rescheduling requests.

Do you offer group discounts?

Yes! Groups of 6 or more travelers are eligible for special discounted rates. Contact us for a custom quote.

Is travel insurance included?

Travel insurance is not included by default in most packages, but can be added during checkout for an additional fee.

How can I track my booking?

Once logged in, visit 'My Trips' from the navigation menu to see all your bookings, payment status, and travel dates in one dashboard.
